02 / OUR STORY

Big imagination.
A human touch.

Entertainment starts with a spark. We want to turn that spark into experiences people enjoy returning to.

Based in Kavala, Greece, Jamsie Entertainment brings together games and digital entertainment under one creative roof. Our projects explore different ways to play, express yourself and connect.

We’re building with curiosity, listening to feedback and improving as we go. Whether you’re a player, a creator or a potential collaborator, you’re welcome here.
